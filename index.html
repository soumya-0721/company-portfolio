01 — Who we are

Two divisions. One standard of care.

NEXT360 Organic Products Pvt. Ltd. is a Hyderabad-based company working across two everyday needs: the food and personal care products people bring into their homes, and the technology businesses rely on to grow.

Through NEXT360 Organic, we offer organic groceries and everyday essentials through an online marketplace. Through NEXT360 IT Services, we design and build websites, mobile applications and custom digital solutions. Different disciplines, the same approach: understand the need, be clear about the scope and deliver work we can stand behind.
