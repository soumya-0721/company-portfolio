Who we work with

The right fit matters more than the size of the logo.

We are interested in partnerships where expectations are clear, responsibilities are shared and both sides can support the relationship properly.

Retailers & distributors

Businesses with a clear route to market and a genuine interest in organic products.

Corporate buyers

Organisations looking for considered gifting, pantry or employee wellness options.

Technology collaborators

Agencies, studios and specialists who want a dependable development partner.

Mission-aligned organisations

Groups whose values and long-term aims align with responsible, transparent growth.

Submitting an enquiry does not create a partnership. Every collaboration is subject to fit, due diligence and a written agreement.

How it begins

A straightforward first conversation.

No pressure and no lengthy pitch. We start by understanding what each side wants to achieve.

01 — Enquire

Tell us about your organisation

Share the model you are interested in, your market and what you are hoping to build.

02 — Talk

An introductory conversation

We discuss expectations, capabilities, timelines and whether there is a sensible fit.

03 — Review

Fit & due diligence

We review the practical details, responsibilities and any information needed before proceeding.

04 — Agree

Put it in writing

If both sides are aligned, the arrangement is documented before work together begins.
