About NEXT360

A Hyderabad company built across nature and technology.

NEXT360 Organic Products Pvt. Ltd. works where everyday needs meet thoughtful execution — in the food and personal care products people choose, and in the digital systems businesses depend on.

Our story Contact Our Team

Who we are

A company with two working parts.

NEXT360 is a growing Indian company with two active business divisions: NEXT360 Organic and NEXT360 IT Services. The first serves households with organic groceries, everyday essentials and personal care products. The second serves businesses with websites, mobile applications and custom digital solutions.

We are deliberately building a company that can do a few things well. That means being specific about what we offer, honest about what is still being developed, and careful about the commitments we make to customers, clients and partners.

Our base is Hyderabad, Telangana. Our perspective is national, and our standard is simple: the work should be useful long after the first conversation ends.

Our vision

To be a trusted Indian company known for products and technology that last.

We want NEXT360 to be remembered for reliable everyday products, thoughtful digital work and relationships that are built on clarity rather than noise.

Our mission

To make organic essentials easier to access and technology easier to use.

We do this by curating useful product categories, supporting customers through clear product enquiries, and building digital solutions around real business requirements.

How we workListen · Define · Deliver

Our approach

Listen first. Then build.

Whether the brief is a product list or a software requirement, the starting point is the same: understand the need before recommending a solution.

Understand the need

We ask about the outcome you want, the people it serves and the constraints you already know about.

Define the scope

We turn the conversation into a clear list of what will be delivered, what is out of scope and what happens next.

Deliver with care

We keep the work practical, test what we build and stay transparent if a requirement needs to change.

Stay accountable

Our relationship does not end at handover. Support and follow-up are agreed as part of the work.

Our values

What guides the decisions we make.

These are working principles, not slogans. They shape how we select products, scope projects and communicate with the people we serve.

01

Honesty

We do not describe a product, certification or capability as something it is not.

02

Care

We choose what we offer with the end user's everyday experience in mind.

03

Clarity

Requirements, scope and next steps should be easy to understand before work begins.

04

Curiosity

We stay open to better methods, new categories and practical improvements.

05

Responsibility

We take ownership of the work we agree to do and the way we communicate it.

06

Partnership

We value relationships that can grow over years rather than one-off transactions.
